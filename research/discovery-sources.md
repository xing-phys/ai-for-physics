## Computational and derivation verification

Executed every Python block in all five chapters with `.venv/bin/python`, NumPy and Matplotlib. All assertions passed. Representative results:

- Chapter 17: prior-generated 95% posterior interval coverage 0.949 across 4,000 experiments; mean rank 24.69 for ranks 0–49.
- Chapter 18: complete 15-call GP/UCB campaign selected a best measured setting at 0.72 in the seeded simulated resonance.
- Chapter 19: sparse identification exactly recovered the supplied noiseless cubic-force coefficients; held-out rollout and monotonic energy assertions passed.
- Chapter 20: empirical CRPS 0.5678 for the appropriately dispersed ensemble versus 0.6902 for an underdispersed one. Energy-error/fidelity example checked.
- Chapter 21: analytic variational-energy gradient agreed with finite differences; optimized six-spin Jastrow energy −7.5780 lay above exact −7.7274 and below the uniform-state baseline; fidelity 0.9531. Exact diffusion mean conservation, semigroup identity and inferred D=0.3 assertions passed.

Manually checked the Gaussian posterior by completing the square; Fisher nuisance Schur complement; qubit binomial score and information; GP conditioning through independent residual; expected improvement integral; Gaussian entropy/rank-one information gain; Bellman two-state values; integration-by-parts boundary terms; quantum gap-fidelity inequality; and diffusion stability multiplier. Scope limitation: these are verified pedagogical examples, not replications of frontier-scale papers or evidence that a large learned model reproduces all physical regimes.

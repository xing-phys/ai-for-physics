## Scope and source selection

The quantum sequence is organized around representation, expectation estimation, optimization, dynamics, exchange statistics, and physical mixed-state inference. Primary papers establish representative methods; a tensor-network review supplies the principal competing framework; a verified 2026 perspective supplies a dated map of unresolved issues. Researcher identity was not used to rank coverage. Di Luo and collaborators enter where backflow, open-system probabilities, and gauge–fermion composition are conceptually relevant.

The requested Google Scholar profile was attempted directly but returned HTTP 429. This did not prevent identifying relevant work through accessible arXiv author/title records and publisher pages. Broad secondary search results and AI-generated research summaries were not used as scientific evidence. No claim that a model “solves the many-body problem” in general is made.

## Derivation and computation checks

- All spin Hamiltonians specify Pauli-matrix conventions and boundary conditions. The two-spin calculation is one bond; periodic chains use one bond per site with at least three sites.
- The energy gradient uses real parameters and equals twice the real covariance force. Imaginary-time SR uses the force without that factor two. The real-coordinate McLachlan real-time equation uses the imaginary covariance, while holomorphic complex TDVP uses the full Hermitian metric and a factor `-i/hbar`.
- The distinction between real-coordinate least-squares dynamics and complex-tangent energy conservation is explicit. A real-only qubit ansatz demonstrates the missing phase direction.
- The local-energy variance identity assumes suitable support and finite moments. A discrete-node counterexample is supplied, preventing an incorrect unconditional zero-variance claim.
- The continuum kinetic formula uses `(grad f) dot (grad f)` without complex conjugation. Coulomb cusp factors are derived in their stated coordinates.
- The tetrahedral POVM inversion is checked algebraically and numerically. An arbitrary normalized outcome vector explicitly reconstructs eigenvalues `(-1, 2)`, demonstrating the physical positivity constraint.
- Seven Python cells were executed in the project virtual environment using NumPy and Matplotlib only. All deterministic assertions passed. The complete four-spin RBM SR training reached energy `-4.75585178`, agreeing with the independent dense eigensolver to the displayed digits. This small-system result is not a scaling claim.
- The four-spin quench's Runge–Kutta trajectory differed from spectral propagation by at most approximately `2.03e-7` over the stated interval, with norm and energy assertions passing. Figures include Schmidt weights, RBM training, and quench observables/entropy.
